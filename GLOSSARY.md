# Glossary

**New Voter**: Someone with little political knowledge who wants to start voting. The site's audience. Not tied to any demographic group.

**Race**: One contest for one office in one state in the 2026 midterm election (e.g. "Georgia U.S. Senate 2026"). v1 covers U.S. Senate and governor races only.

**Candidate**: A person who appeared on the ballot in a Race.

**Result**: The outcome of a Race: who won, or that it is still uncalled.

**Lesson**: Evergreen educational content (how voting works, what an office does) that stays useful after the 2026 election.
