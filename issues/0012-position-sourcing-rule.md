## Question

What standard must a candidate position meet before the site shows it?

## Resolution

A position is shown only if an agent opened the cited page and the sentence says no more than the page does. Found during the build: first-pass research wrote positions whose cited page did not support them, and a full sweep reworded roughly a quarter of all positions and deleted a handful. Rule going forward: one writer per race file at a time (a concurrent write lost five races of work once), every position re-opened before publish, fewer true positions over more doubtful ones. Instructions live in data/PROFILE-PASS.md and data/SOURCE-SWEEP.md.
